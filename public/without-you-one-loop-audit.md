# Without You
## One-loop audit checklist

MyGrokFlow. Free worksheet. Not a prompt pack.

For founders, software engineers, and operators who already pay for time or a team, and still have one workflow that dies if they skip a day.

If you want a $99 chatbot, or you are “just exploring AI,” this page will not help. Close it.

### 1. Name the loop (one)

Do not audit the company. Pick the job that still needs you.

A loop is a job that happens more than once a week, has a clear start and a clear “done,” and currently waits on your judgment, login, copy-paste, or “I’ll do it later.”

If you cannot name one loop, you do not have an automation problem. You have a priority problem. Stop here.

### 2. The without-you test

Yes or no. Be unkind.

- [ ] If I disappear for five workdays, this loop slips or stops.
- [ ] The trigger is a person (me, a client, a teammate), not a system event.
- [ ] I re-enter the same facts in two or more places.
- [ ] “Done” is me saying it’s done, not a recorded state.
- [ ] Someone asks me “did this go out?” at least weekly.
- [ ] A cheaper person could do most of the steps if I wrote the rules down. I just haven’t.
- [ ] A tool already exists for part of this, and I am the glue.
- [ ] I have tried a prompt, a zap, or a hire, and the loop still sits on me.

**Yes count**

- **0–2** Annoying, not expensive. Leave it.
- **3–5** Candidate. You are the integration.
- **6–8** This is the one. It is already costing calendar, not theory.

No industry benchmarks on this page. Your calendar is the proof.

### 3. Worth replacing, or just loud?

Keep going only if you scored 3+.

- [ ] This loop already costs paid time (yours at your rate, or a teammate’s).
- [ ] There is a decider who can say yes to replacing it.
- [ ] A missed week creates a real mess (money, a client, a ship date), not just a messy inbox.
- [ ] I can describe the happy path in eight steps or fewer. (If it takes a novel, it is not one loop.)

If any of these is no, pick a smaller loop and start again.

### 4. What “without you” actually means

**Not:** a prompt you still paste. A dashboard you still babysit. A chatbot for FAQs you do not have.

**Yes:** a system that takes the trigger, does the work, and leaves a record, with you only on exceptions.

If you cannot name the exception rule, you are not ready to automate. Write the rule first.
